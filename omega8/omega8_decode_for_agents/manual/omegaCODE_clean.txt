7 TUNING 
! 
For the Omega series, we developed a very accurate, but not perfect, software routine of computer-corrected Oscillator 
tuning. It looks at each note and corrects it to a pre-de ned value. The goal was to keep all 16 Oscillators in near 
perfect tune over the wide range of the keyboard, but not so perfect as to eliminate the element of randomness, which 
is the nature, and beauty of analog synthesis. There is some tempering at the very low and high pitch ranges. Adjust as 
needed. 
The tuning of the lters is done in hardware and is not part of the Tune routine. You may hear with each voice a very 
slight di erence in timbre or tone; this is not a fault of our design. Rather, we have chosen to be faithful and follow the 
chosen path, blazed by our history shaping predecessors, manufacturers of the classic mono/polyphonic analog 
synthesizers of the past. It is precisely this slight variance from voice to voice that gives discrete and Curtis chip based 
analog synthesizers their vibrancy and soul-pleasing warmth and character. 
Occasionally, after the Tune procedure, some of the voices may return in pitch a half step o . The tuning procedure 
works best when performed in a ROM sound. We have created a special patch for this procedure: B128. Set to patch 
B128, press a low note on your MIDI keyboard and run the tuning procedure again.

PAN POSITIONS IN A MULTI 
The Multi preset stores its own Pan Position, Pan Rate, and Pan Depth for each voice. Waveform, key trigger, and MIDI 
sync information is carried into the Multi preset from the patches used in the Multi preset. 
Note: Edits made to patches while in the Multi bank are savable. The Multi part you are in determines which patch you 
are editing and saving. Edits made to the patch must be saved before you go to another Multi part otherwise the edits 
are lost. Press save and select RAM location you want to save the patch to. 
DETERMINING MIDI CHANNELS IN A CHANNELIZED MULTI PATCH 
The rst Multi part is received on the current MIDI channel that the Omega is set to, additional parts receive on 
successive MIDI channels. Example: if the unit is set to MIDI channel 3, Multi part 2 receives on channel 4, Multi part 3 
receives on channel 5, etc.

9d OSCILLATORS 
! 
Trust and Bless the Source 
Oscillators are the biogenesis of Analog Synthesis, 
producing periodic or regularly repeating waveforms
 familiar pitched sounds when in the right ranges. 
Tuning controls alter pitch of the Oscillators; 
waveform selectors, Pulse Width, and Sync controls 
craft the harmonic spectrum of the signal its basic 
timbre, or tone coloration. Vibration therapy at best. 
KNOBS 
FREQUENCY: Adjusts Oscillator pitch or tuning. 
PULSE WIDTH: Adjusts width of Square wave near 
perfect, rubbery square at 12:00, and an ideal sweet 
spot for deep, soul-sweeping modulation by an LFO. 
OSCILLATOR LEVEL: Adjusts Oscillators mix. 
NOISE: Adjusts White Noise ( at noise) mix level. 
SWITCHES 
SINE (CODE), TRIANGLE, SAWTOOTH, SQUARE: 
Combinable Oscillator waveforms can transform the 
single harmonic SINE with fully-loaded sawtooth (has 
all harmonics of the fundamental pitch), and odd 
harmonic square (at 50% of its duty cycle), into gold. 
SUB: Oscillator 1 subharmonic waveform: A divided 
frequency below the fundamental in a ratio of 1/x. 
SYNC: Hard syncs frequency of Oscillator 2 to 
Oscillator 1 frequency, so OSC 1 synchronizes the phase or cycle of OSC 2, resulting in the same base frequency. 
Amplitude modulation results when the two oscillators are near the same frequency, and when near identical, a, 
phase-shifted vibe. Higher OSC 2 freqs. will dramatically color OSC 1 s pitch & timbre. 
 EDIT 
Page 1: Master Tune, Oscillator 1 and 2 Frequency. When in this page the Q dial may also be used to edit 
Oscillator frequencies. 
Page 2: [FINE] - Oscillator 2 ne tuning, [MODE] - Oscillator 2 pitch modes, [ENV1] - Amount of Oscillator 2 
Frequency modulated by envelope 1.

9e FILTERS 
! 
You Know What You Want to Hype or Silence 
The Filter Frequency attenuates the higher frequency components those which lie above the 
adjustable cuto frequency and passes the lower frequency components of the audio signal.
